How it works

Show your hand to the camera and hold the gesture for about one second. SignBridge detects the hand landmarks and converts recognized gestures into words or letters. Nothing is spoken until you press Speak Output.

In Air Draw mode, raise your index finger to draw in the air. An open hand pauses drawing and a closed fist works as an eraser.
